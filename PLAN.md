# Setline plan

All five phases from SPEC.md section 7 are done. Work now happens as fixes and polish the user asks for. The current version is in `js/version.js` (1.60.0). The release log, including releases that were later undone, is in `docs/HISTORY.md`; read it only when you need the story behind something.

## What's in the app now

**Look.** Headlines in two weights (the Today greeting's first word bold, the rest lighter and softer); page titles arrive word by word, each fading in and rising a touch; buttons are full pills. Records on Progress carry a glossy medal (gold for one set in the last week), and a record tile tilts towards your finger with a shine sliding across it when pressed.

**Motion from Libraries.dev** (ported to plain JS/CSS, MIT, notices kept in the files): a border beam travels round Today's card while a workout or run is live. On the voice sheet a soft light along the bottom rises and brightens with your voice (the recorder's own level, nothing while the mic is off) and breathes while the words are worked out; since 1.60 it is one still gradient that only fades and scales, not the ported voice glow's masked layers. All transform/opacity or plain canvas, paused when hidden or off screen, still with reduced motion. The motion and performance budget is in CLAUDE.md.

**The Orb** is a sphere of lit dots turning slowly in 3D, in the theme's colours (accent up top, blue, violet round the bottom), drawn on a canvas (`js/ui/dotorb.js`). Every orb is one (the dock, the message box, the voice sheet, onboarding, the interview), and they all turn together on one clock and one spin, so whenever one hands over to another (flying up from the dock into the voice sheet, from the sheet into the message box, landing back) the dots are exactly where they were and nothing jumps; only one orb shows at a time. Each dot is stamped from one sheet of pre-drawn dots (no gradients or shadows per dot), at most 2× the screen's pixel density, on the app's one animation frame (`js/ui/frame.js`). At rest it breathes; while you talk it swells and ripples with your voice (the lows round its middle, the highs at its poles); while it thinks it turns faster and a bright meridian sweeps round it; while the Coach speaks it pulses with the voice. At rest it's drawn at half rate, hidden orbs only now and then, and with reduced motion it holds still.

**Look (1.59, "deep water glass").** Manrope; a deep-water base with soft, still light in the sea behind every screen (in the theme's colours; ocean, mint on deep blue, is the house theme); cards are tinted (solid, not blurred, since 1.60) with a rim of light; labels in sentence case. Today's Up next is the hero: the session's name big, next time's weights as big numbers and Start workout.

**Navigation.** A split floating bar: Today, Train, Food, You in one capsule of frosted glass, the orb in its own drop of glass beside it; the chosen tab's icon lifts once and settles. Tapping the orb blooms the Coach open over the whole screen; holding it opens the voice sheet straight away (the orb gives under your finger, the sheet's background fades in and the orb flies up to its middle) and you talk while you hold. Settings opens from the icon on Today and from You. The status bar is the app's own colour; when content scrolls under it, a short gradient just below it (barely noticeable) blends it into the page; it also takes the Coach's, the voice screen's and an open sheet's colour. The bar at the bottom is bright-rimmed frosted glass (the bar and the message box are the only blurred surfaces, at most 16 px, and they turn solid while screens move), with a soft gradient that deepens the page around it (and follows it when it shrinks to the pill); its indicator slides with a liquid stretch and the chosen icon springs (Customize → Glass: Liquid / Soft / Off; Background glow: On / Soft / Off). Pages and sheets animate in; sheets are iOS-style (the page behind shrinks back) and close with a swipe down. The Android back swipe is respected.

**Today.** Greeting and streak, a note the Coach pinned (missed targets, a stalled lift, the week falling behind), "How do you feel today?" check-in, Up next (follows the weekday plan and one-off day changes, says "· Tomorrow" when relevant, shows next time's target per lift, and offers "Go lighter" after a short night, a low-energy morning or a sport day yesterday), the week strip, week cards (workouts ring, cardio, last session, run, latest record).

**Train.** Start a routine or an empty workout. The library is a full gym (156 exercises with machines, cables, Smith and plate-loaded variations, in English and Danish). Suggested weights follow double progression and are always loadable (a full gym's steps: bars and machines 2.5 kg, dumbbells 2 kg). After each session every lift is checked against its plan. Active workout: steppers, log set, previous performance, set rows that land with a glow and a "New record" chip (plus a warm banner from the top), swipe to delete, rest ring with ±15 and skip, a lock-screen rest notification with "Log 75 kg × 8" and "+15 s" buttons (works without opening the app), exercises reordered by dragging in the Exercises sheet ("Keep for next time" saves that order to the routine), auto-advance, auto warm-ups with a short timer between them that says what's next, plate calculator, wake lock, finish with a hero ring and check, "Save as a routine". Routines: editor (drag exercises into order by their pull tab, or hold a row), starter programs, "Your own program" (pasted text read by the Coach), plans from the Coach. History and progress charts. A finished workout can be deleted from its page (asked once, then Undo; its records are worked out again from what's left). Tapping Start on Up next opens the card's surface out into the workout page; after finishing, today's dot in the week strip fills in with a pop the next time you see it.

**Food.** Say it logs what you ate; questions and small talk go to the Coach instead. Meals split into items, food search (Danish database), barcode and photo scan, favourite meals, "Your usuals" (meals you keep eating together, one tap), "same as yesterday's lunch" by voice or text, protein and calorie targets.

**You.** Profile, progress (with a records wall and your month as a shareable picture), muscles this week ("Add 3 sets" to the running workout when a group is behind), body (bodyweight with a smoothed trend and weekly rate, measurements, monthly photos), history, what the Coach remembers, customize (themes: violet, the two-tone aurora / sunset / ocean with two-colour buttons, orb and glow, slate, sage, sand, clay, mono), settings (keys, voice, units, backup, Google Drive backup; weight steps are the app's own; sections dragged into your own order by their pull tabs), and "Report a bug or idea" (also one tap from the flag on a voice result card or under any Coach reply: pick what went wrong and it's sent with what was heard and done): kept on the phone and sent as a GitHub issue with the version, screen, phone, the last things heard, voice commands you undid right away and recent errors (also by saying or typing "bug: …" / "idea: …" anywhere, or the home-screen shortcut).

**Voice.** Groq Whisper speech-to-text (the accurate large-v3 model; each recording is cleaned for a loud gym first: rumble filtered, the noise around the words cut, level evened out, silence never sent) with junk filtering (including Whisper repeating its own prompt on silence) and a Danish re-run; a local English/Danish parser that matches exercise names word by word and forgives mishearings ("preacher kill" is a preacher curl; one in today's workout wins a close call; a name it doesn't know is asked about, with "Add …", never guessed); saying a name logs on that exercise (added if needed), no name means the one you're on (sets in any order, "next set was only nine reps", "set one is done but only nine reps", "same again", "one more rep", …); Gemini fallback for anything else. Spoken replies use Gemini's natural voice by default (30 voices), starting the moment the answer is in (the first sentence is voiced while the reply is still written), with short quota pauses and the phone's own voice as a fallback. The mic never reopens by itself after a reply. Hands-free and quick words: "done" (the planned set), "done, 7" / "got 7" (7 reps at the planned weight), "that felt heavy" / "easy" (the rest of that lift's sets 2.5 kg lighter or heavier), "yes" / "do it" (confirms the card waiting, or takes the Coach's offer); "finish" still ends the workout. The voice sheet is the only listening screen (the small floating orb is gone since 1.60): its top bar and controls stay put the whole time, its pill says "Opening mic" then "Listening", labels cross-fade in place ("Release to send" → "Hold to talk"), what you said appears word by word, and "Working it out" shows only when that takes over 0.6 s (then for at least 0.8 s). Asking the Coach something there goes straight to the Coach: it is laid out under the sheet, the sheet fades and its orb flies into the message box, and Home never shows in between.

**Coach.** Streaming chat that knows your log (with the latest sessions), routines, food (today and the last 7 days), settings and the next 7 days, plus a compact base of training science (progression, volume, deloads, fat loss and gain, sleep, lifting next to wrestling), and runs the app for you. It keeps an eye on your goals: after a session that missed most of its plan, a lift that stalls, or a week that can't reach its goal, it pins a note (Coach and Today) you can talk through or put away. It remembers facts (`REMEMBER:` lines) and makes changes with `CHANGE:` lines only when you tell it to, always shown under the reply with Undo: plan changes (a day becomes Wrestling or rest, routines move days, exercises are swapped, added or removed), settings (rest, weekly goal, units, theme, spoken replies, language, haptics, …), anything the app understands from words (log food, water, weight, cardio, sets, targets, start a routine) and opening a page. It answers questions about the app from its built-in guide. When it suggests something you haven't asked for (moving legs away from wrestling, a calorie change when the weight trend is off, a lighter jump), it offers it as up to two "Do it" chips under the reply (one tap applies it with Undo; saying or typing "yes" / "do it" takes the first; they step aside while you type). The Coach uses the newest Flash with a little thinking (quick and smart). Talking through the message-box orb: it answers aloud, and listens again by itself only when its answer ended with a question. Tapping the orb fades the Coach in while the orb flies in a straight line from the bar (no trail), turning into the message box's orb on the way, and lands in the message box, which gives under it and springs back up a little as a soft light spreads through it, with one haptic tap; closing flies it home the same way, the bar taking it with a faint ring of light. Sending a typed message lifts the words out of the box right where you typed them as a bubble that rises on a soft curve and settles exactly onto its place in the chat (its landing spot is tracked every frame, so the keyboard going down or the thread moving can't make it jump), while the conversation above glides up to make room. Replies flow in one word at a time at a steady pace that eases up or down with how much has arrived (never in bursts, never stopping dead in a pause), each word fading in; the bubble the reply streams into becomes the finished message (same width, nothing re-wraps or shows twice), messages already on screen are never redrawn, and at most one change reaches the page per frame. The app's audio switches off the moment nothing records or plays, so music in your headphones comes straight back. While it thinks, the message box says what it's doing ("Thinking…", "Reading your log…", "Putting it together…"), each phrase fading in letter by letter along a colour gradient in the box's one placeholder line, which cross-fades back to "Ask your coach"; the Do it chips fade in without moving the thread. When the answer starts, the box's orb pulses once and the first line rises out of the box; while it speaks, the orb swells with its voice and the sentence being spoken stays bright while the rest dims a little. The orb's landing also sends a soft light through the box. The thread keeps to the end of the reply only while you're within about 80 px of it. The dock orb gives under your finger. The orb is one light everywhere: during a rest a thin ring around the dock orb drains to the end of it and the orb glows "go" when it's over; a set logged by voice sends a spark from the orb to its row (gold, with a gold flare on the orb, for a record, which a tapped record gets too); when it didn't understand, the orb shakes its head.
