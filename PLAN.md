# Setline plan

All five phases from SPEC.md section 7 are done. Work now happens as fixes and polish the user asks for. The current version is in `js/version.js` (1.49.0). The release log, including releases that were later undone, is in `docs/HISTORY.md`; read it only when you need the story behind something.

## What's in the app now

**Navigation.** A floating glass dock: Today, Train, the orb in the middle, Food, You. Tapping the orb blooms the Coach open over the whole screen; holding it is hold-to-talk for a voice command. Settings opens from the icon on Today and from You. The status bar is the app's own colour; when content scrolls under it, a short gradient just below it (barely noticeable) blends it into the page. The bar at the bottom is clear, bright-rimmed live glass with a soft gradient blur that feathers out around the bar only (and follows it when it shrinks to the pill); its indicator slides with a liquid stretch and the chosen icon springs (Customize → Glass: Liquid / Soft / Off; Background glow: On / Soft / Off). Pages and sheets animate in; sheets are iOS-style (the page behind shrinks back) and close with a swipe down. The Android back swipe is respected.

**Today.** Greeting and streak, a note the Coach pinned (missed targets, a stalled lift, the week falling behind), "How do you feel today?" check-in, Up next (follows the weekday plan and one-off day changes, says "· Tomorrow" when relevant, and shows next time's target per lift), the week strip, week cards (workouts ring, cardio, last session, run, latest record).

**Train.** Start a routine or an empty workout. Suggested weights follow double progression and are always loadable (bar + plate pairs, dumbbell steps, your steps). After each session every lift is checked against its plan. Active workout: steppers, log set, previous performance, set rows that land with a glow and a "New record" chip (plus a warm banner from the top), swipe to delete, rest ring with ±15 and skip, a lock-screen rest notification with "Log 75 kg × 8" and "+15 s" buttons (works without opening the app), auto-advance, auto warm-ups, plate calculator, wake lock, finish with a hero ring and check, "Save as a routine". Routines: editor, starter programs, "Your own program" (pasted text read by the Coach), plans from the Coach. History and progress charts. Tapping Start on Up next opens the card's surface out into the workout page; after finishing, today's dot in the week strip fills in with a pop the next time you see it.

**Food.** Say it logs what you ate; questions and small talk go to the Coach instead. Meals split into items, food search (Danish database), barcode and photo scan, favourite meals, "Your usuals" (meals you keep eating together, one tap), "same as yesterday's lunch" by voice or text, protein and calorie targets.

**You.** Profile, progress (with a records wall), body (bodyweight with a smoothed trend and weekly rate, measurements, monthly photos), history, what the Coach remembers, customize (themes: violet, the two-tone aurora / sunset / ocean with two-colour buttons, orb and glow, slate, sage, sand, clay, mono), settings (keys, voice, units, backup, Google Drive backup).

**Voice.** Groq Whisper speech-to-text with junk filtering and a Danish re-run; a local English/Danish parser (sets in any order, "set one is done but only nine reps", "same again", "one more rep", …); Gemini fallback for anything else. Spoken replies use Gemini's natural voice by default, with short quota pauses and the phone's own voice as a fallback. The mic never reopens by itself after a reply. Hands-free and quick words: "done" (the planned set), "done, 7" / "got 7" (7 reps at the planned weight), "that felt heavy" / "easy" (the rest of that lift's sets 2.5 kg lighter or heavier), "yes" / "do it" (confirms the card waiting, or takes the Coach's offer); "finish" still ends the workout.

**Coach.** Streaming chat that knows your log (with the latest sessions), routines, food (today and the last 7 days), settings and the next 7 days, plus a compact base of training science (progression, volume, deloads, fat loss and gain, sleep, lifting next to wrestling), and runs the app for you. It keeps an eye on your goals: after a session that missed most of its plan, a lift that stalls, or a week that can't reach its goal, it pins a note (Coach and Today) you can talk through or put away. It remembers facts (`REMEMBER:` lines) and makes changes with `CHANGE:` lines only when you tell it to, always shown under the reply with Undo: plan changes (a day becomes Wrestling or rest, routines move days, exercises are swapped, added or removed), settings (rest, weekly goal, units, theme, spoken replies, language, haptics, …), anything the app understands from words (log food, water, weight, cardio, sets, targets, start a routine) and opening a page. It answers questions about the app from its built-in guide. When it suggests something you haven't asked for (moving legs away from wrestling, a calorie change when the weight trend is off, a lighter jump), it offers it as up to two "Do it" chips under the reply (one tap applies it with Undo; saying or typing "yes" / "do it" takes the first; they step aside while you type). The Coach uses the newest Flash with a little thinking (quick and smart); Settings → Voice → Coach brain can switch to the newest Pro (smartest, a few seconds slower). Talking through the message-box orb: it answers aloud, and listens again by itself only when its answer ended with a question. Tapping the orb blooms the Coach open with a small ripple while the orb shoots off in a straight line from the bar, slows just before the message box and hits it (a deep squash and wobble, a double shockwave, light flooding the box, the box pushed down with a little shake, one smooth haptic tap); closing flies it home. Sending a typed message lifts it out of the box as a bubble on a spring while the conversation above glides up to make room. Replies stream in line by line (finished lines are drawn once), each word blurring in. While it thinks, soft gradients drift inside the message box and clouds move in the background. When the answer starts, the box's orb pulses once and the first line rises out of the box; while it speaks, the orb swells with its voice and the sentence being spoken stays bright while the rest dims a little. The orb's landing also sends a soft light through the box. Reply words blur in and the thread glides down after them; touching the thread stops the follow. The dock orb gives under your finger.
