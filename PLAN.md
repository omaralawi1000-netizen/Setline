## What's in the app now

**Navigation.** A floating glass dock: Today, Train, the orb in the middle, Food, You. Tapping the orb blooms the Coach open over the whole screen; holding it is hold-to-talk for a voice command. Settings opens from the icon on Today and from You. The status bar is the app's own colour; when content scrolls under it, a short gradient just below it (barely noticeable) blends it into the page; it also takes the Coach's, the voice screen's and an open sheet's colour. The bar at the bottom is clear, bright-rimmed live glass with a wide, soft gradient blur that feathers out around the bar (and follows it when it shrinks to the pill); its indicator slides with a liquid stretch and the chosen icon springs (Customize → Glass: Liquid / Soft / Off; Background glow: On / Soft / Off). Pages and sheets animate in; sheets are iOS-style (the page behind shrinks back) and close with a swipe down. The Android back swipe is respected.

**Today.** Greeting and streak, a note the Coach pinned (missed targets, a stalled lift, the week falling behind), "How do you feel today?" check-in, Up next (follows the weekday plan and one-off day changes, says "· Tomorrow" when relevant, shows next time's target per lift, and offers "Go lighter" after a short night, a low-energy morning or a sport day yesterday), the week strip, week cards (workouts ring, cardio, last session, run, latest record).

**Train.** Start a routine or an empty workout. The library is a full gym (156 exercises with machines, cables, Smith and plate-loaded variations, in English and Danish). Suggested weights follow double progression and are always loadable (a full gym's steps: bars and machines 2.5 kg, dumbbells 2 kg). After each session every lift is checked against its plan. Active workout: steppers, log set, previous performance, set rows that land with a glow and a "New record" chip (plus a warm banner from the top), swipe to delete, rest ring with ±15 and skip, a lock-screen rest notification with "Log 75 kg × 8" and "+15 s" buttons (works without opening the app), exercises reordered by dragging in the Exercises sheet ("Keep for next time" saves that order to the routine), auto-advance, auto warm-ups with a short timer between them that says what's next, plate calculator, wake lock, finish with a hero ring and check, "Save as a routine". Routines: editor (drag exercises into order by their pull tab, or hold a row), starter programs, "Your own program" (pasted text read by the Coach), plans from the Coach. History and progress charts. A finished workout can be deleted from its page (asked once, then Undo; its records are worked out again from what's left). Tapping Start on Up next opens the card's surface out into the workout page; after finishing, today's dot in the week strip fills in with a pop the next time you see it.

**Food.** Say it logs what you ate; questions and small talk go to the Coach instead. Meals split into items, food search (Danish database), barcode and photo scan, favourite meals, "Your usuals" (meals you keep eating together, one tap), "same as yesterday's lunch" by voice or text, protein and calorie targets.

**You.** Profile, progress (with a records wall and your month as a shareable picture), muscles this week ("Add 3 sets" to the running workout when a group is behind), body (bodyweight with a smoothed trend and weekly rate, measurements, monthly photos), history, what the Coach remembers, customize (themes: violet, the two-tone aurora / sunset / ocean with two-colour buttons, orb and glow, slate, sage, sand, clay, mono), settings (keys, voice, units, backup, Google Drive backup; weight steps are the app's own; sections dragged into your own order by their pull tabs), and "Report a bug or idea" (also one tap from the flag on a voice result card or under any Coach reply: pick what went wrong and it's sent with what was heard and done): kept on the phone and sent as a GitHub issue with the version, screen, phone, the last things heard, voice commands you undid right away and recent errors (also by saying or typing "bug: …" / "idea: …" anywhere, or the home-screen shortcut).

**Voice.** Groq Whisper speech-to-text (the accurate large-v3 model; each recording is cleaned for a loud gym first: rumble filtered, the noise around the words cut, level evened out, silence never sent) with junk filtering (including Whisper repeating its own prompt on silence) and a Danish re-run; a local English/Danish parser that matches exercise names word by word and forgives mishearings ("preacher kill" is a preacher curl; one in today's workout wins a close call; a name it doesn't know is asked about, with "Add …", never guessed); saying a name logs on that exercise (added if needed), no name means the one you're on (sets in any order, "next set was only nine reps", "set one is done but only nine reps", "same again", "one more rep", …); Gemini fallback for anything else. Spoken replies use Gemini's natural voice by default (30 voices), starting the moment the answer is in (the first sentence is voiced while the reply is still written), with short quota pauses and the phone's own voice as a fallback. The mic never reopens by itself after a reply. Hands-free and quick words: "done" (the planned set), "done, 7" / "got 7" (7 reps at the planned weight), "that felt heavy" / "easy" (the rest of that lift's sets 2.5 kg lighter or heavier), "yes" / "do it" (confirms the card waiting, or takes the Coach's offer); "finish" still ends the workout.

**Coach.** Streaming chat that knows your log (with the latest sessions), routines, food (today and the last 7 days), settings and the next 7 days, plus a compact base of training science (progression, volume, deloads, fat loss and gain, sleep, lifting next to wrestling), and runs the app for you. It keeps an eye on your goals: after a session that missed most of its plan, a lift that stalls, or a week that can't reach its goal, it pins a note (Coach and Today) you can talk through or put away. It remembers facts (`REMEMBER:` lines) and makes changes with `CHANGE:` lines only when you tell it to, always shown under the reply with Undo: plan changes (a day becomes Wrestling or rest, routines move days, exercises are swapped, added or removed), settings (rest, weekly goal, units, theme, spoken replies, language, haptics, …), anything the app understands from words (log food, water, weight, cardio, sets, targets, start a routine) and opening a page. It answers questions about the app from its built-in guide. When it suggests something you haven't asked for (moving legs away from wrestling, a calorie change when the weight trend is off, a lighter jump), it offers it as up to two "Do it" chips under the reply (one tap applies it with Undo; saying or typing "yes" / "do it" takes the first; they step aside while you type). The Coach uses the newest Flash with a little thinking (quick and smart). Talking through the message-box orb: it answers aloud, and listens again by itself only when its answer ended with a question. Tapping the orb blooms the Coach open with a small ripple while the orb shoots off in a straight line from the bar, staying sharp with a soft comet of its own light trailing behind it (longest when it's fastest, drawn back in as it lands), turning into the message box's orb on the way, and glides to a stop exactly in its place in the message box (no squash, bounce or shake: a soft light spreads through the box and one light haptic tap); closing flies it home the same way, the bar taking it with a faint ring of light. Sending a typed message lifts the words out of the box right where you typed them as a bubble that rises on a soft curve and settles exactly onto its place in the chat (its landing spot is tracked every frame, so the keyboard going down or the thread moving can't make it jump), while the conversation above glides up to make room. Replies flow in one word at a time at a steady pace that eases up or down with how much has arrived (never in bursts, never stopping dead in a pause), each word blurring in and rising a touch (a soft blurred leading edge); words already showing are never redrawn, and the flag's place beside the reply is kept free so the text doesn't re-wrap when it finishes. The app's audio switches off the moment nothing records or plays, so music in your headphones comes straight back. While it thinks, the message box says what it's doing ("Thinking…", "Reading your log…", "Putting it together…"), each phrase blurring in letter by letter along a colour gradient, soft gradients drift inside the box and clouds move in the background. When the answer starts, the box's orb pulses once and the first line rises out of the box; while it speaks, the orb swells with its voice and the sentence being spoken stays bright while the rest dims a little. The orb's landing also sends a soft light through the box. Reply words fade in and the thread glides down after them; touching the thread stops the follow. The dock orb gives under your finger. The orb is one light everywhere: during a rest a thin ring around the dock orb drains to the end of it and the orb glows "go" when it's over; a set logged by voice sends a spark from the orb to its row (gold, with a gold flare on the orb, for a record, which a tapped record gets too); when it didn't understand, the orb shakes its head; while it thinks, a slow swirl turns inside it; while you talk, the body of your voice swells its core and the sharp sounds light its rim; now and then a glint of light crosses the dock orb. The voice screen's orb leaves the same light trail flying up and back into the bar.
